LCPS Policy 5030 Highlights

1. Category Weighting: Major Summative assessments are weighted at 60%, Minor Assessments at 30%, and Formative tasks at 10%.

2. Summative Retake Rule: Students scoring below 70% on major summatives are eligible for a retake (grade capped at a max of 70%).

3. Grading Scale & Quality Points:

A+: 98-100 (4.3)
A: 93-97 (4.0)
A-: 90-92 (3.7)
B+: 87-89 (3.3)
B: 83-86 (3.0)
B-: 80-82 (2.7)
C+: 77-79 (2.3)
C: 73-76 (2.0)
C-: 70-72 (1.7)
D+: 67-69 (1.3)
D: 63-66 (1.0)
D-: 60-62 (0.7)
